6. `scripts/ga_gate.py:48` removes PYTHONPATH/PYTHONHOME/VIRTUAL_ENV/Conda selection from child environment. Consequently a launcher-only Python hook injected by PYTHONPATH is not an inherited, proven boundary for all uv/gate/native descendants. This audit adds no broker, monkeypatch of product permissions, or new skip.
